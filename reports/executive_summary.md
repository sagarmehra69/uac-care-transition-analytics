## Overview

The **UAC Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcome Analytics** project was developed to analyze, monitor, and forecast operational performance within the **Unaccompanied Alien Children (UAC) Program** managed by the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services (HHS).

The project focuses on transforming raw operational data into meaningful analytical insights using:
- Data Analytics
- Machine Learning
- Forecasting
- Interactive Visualization

An interactive dashboard was developed using **Python** and **Streamlit** to help monitor operational efficiency, care-load trends, discharge performance, bottlenecks, and future operational demand.

---

## Purpose of the Project

The primary purpose of the project is to:
- Forecast future HHS care demand
- Monitor operational efficiency
- Detect bottlenecks automatically
- Identify anomalies in operational activity
- Support proactive planning and decision-making

The system was designed to improve operational visibility and reduce reactive decision-making in high-demand care environments.

---

## Key Work Completed

The project included:
- Data preprocessing and cleaning
- KPI engineering
- Bottleneck detection system
- Forecasting using Prophet
- Anomaly detection using Isolation Forest
- Interactive dashboard development
- Automated insights and recommendations

Several operational KPIs were calculated, including:
- Transfer Efficiency
- Discharge Effectiveness
- Backlog Rate
- Pipeline Throughput
- Stability Indicators

---

## Major Findings

The analysis identified several important operational trends:

- Peak HHS care load exceeded **8,000 children** during high-demand periods.
- Transfer counts crossed **300+ daily transfers** during operational surges.
- Reduced discharge effectiveness caused backlog accumulation.
- Sustained bottlenecks increased operational pressure and reduced throughput.
- Forecasting models indicated possible future increases in care demand.
- Anomaly detection identified unusual operational spikes and irregular patterns.
